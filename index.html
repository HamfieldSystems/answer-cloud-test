Questions? Answered.

What can answer.cloud learn from?

Help centers, documentation sites, wikis, shared drives, PDFs and past support tickets. If it's text your team relies on, it can become an answer.

Is my data used to train public models?

No. Your content is only used to answer questions in your own workspace.

What happens when it doesn't know?

It says so — and logs the question so you can see exactly where your content has gaps.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first source and get answers in under ten minutes.
