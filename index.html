Concept notes

What this rewrite changes, and why.

Outcome first
The page now opens with what people want: being found in AI answers, and seeing how AI uses their site. The tools come later.
The prompt is the "how"
The one-prompt build is the way to get the outcome, not the headline. The page makes clear there's nothing to set up: no sign-up, no card, no repo.
Agent instructions off the human page
Today the full agent instructions sit on the homepage. In this concept they'd move to a page only for agents, so people see the pitch and agents still get the steps.
Only real facts
Every feature and price here comes from answer.cloud's current site. There are no testimonials, stats or new claims.
